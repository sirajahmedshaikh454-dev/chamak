Chamak

A truck-art studio. Type a name, shuffle until a poster feels like yours, and download it. Every poster is drawn from a seed, so a link brings back exactly the same one.

Urdu works too: type پاکستان زندہ باد in the first box.

Everything runs in this tab. Nothing is uploaded. The art is generated, not copied from any artist; it borrows the visual vocabulary of Pakistan's painted trucks and buses.
